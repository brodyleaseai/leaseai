# LEASEAI PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date: October 5, 2026**

At LeaseAI, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information.

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

**1.1 Information You Provide**

**Account Information:**
- Name and email address
- Company name and phone number
- Password (encrypted)

**Document Information:**
- Lease documents you upload
- Property addresses
- Tenant names
- Notes and instructions

**Payment Information:**
- Credit card details (processed by Stripe, not stored by us)
- Billing address
- Transaction history

**Communications:**
- Support tickets and emails
- Feedback and survey responses

**1.2 Information We Collect Automatically**

**Usage Data:**
- Pages visited and features used
- Time spent on platform
- Device type and browser
- IP address and location (city/country)
- Referring website

**Cookies and Tracking:**
- Session cookies (required for functionality)
- Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)
- You can disable non-essential cookies in settings

**1.3 Information from Third Parties**

**AI Processing:**
- We use OpenAI GPT-4 to extract lease data
- OpenAI processes document text per their Data Processing Agreement
- OpenAI does not train models on your data (per their enterprise DPA)

**Payment Processing:**
- Stripe processes payments and provides transaction data
- See Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

**Provide Services:**
- Process and abstract your leases
- Generate PDF reports
- Store and manage your documents
- Provide ASC 842 calculations

**Communications:**
- Send service emails (upload confirmations, abstract ready)
- Respond to support requests
- Send account updates and security alerts
- Marketing emails (you can opt-out)

**Improve Services:**
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve AI accuracy
- Debug issues
- Develop new features

**Business Operations:**
- Process payments
- Prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service

**Legal Basis (GDPR):**
- Contract performance (provide services)
- Legitimate interest (improve services, prevent fraud)
- Legal obligation (comply with laws)
- Consent (marketing emails)

## 3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

**3.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data**
We never sell your personal information or documents to third parties.

**3.2 Service Providers**
We share data with trusted providers:

- **Supabase** (database hosting) - stores your account and document data
- **OpenAI** (AI processing) - processes document text for extraction
- **Stripe** (payments) - processes payments and subscriptions
- **SendGrid** (email) - sends service and marketing emails
- **Google Analytics** (analytics) - tracks website usage
- **Vercel/AWS** (hosting) - hosts our application

All providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

**3.3 Legal Requirements**
We may disclose information:
- To comply with laws or court orders
- To enforce our Terms of Service
- To protect our rights or safety
- In connection with merger or acquisition (with notice)

**3.4 Aggregated Data**
We may share anonymized, aggregated statistics (e.g., "90% of leases include CAM charges") that do not identify you.

## 4. DATA SECURITY

**4.1 Security Measures**
- **Encryption:** TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Infrastructure:** SOC 2 Type II compliant providers
- **Audits:** Regular security audits and penetration testing
- **Employee Training:** Security awareness training

**4.2 Your Responsibilities**
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Keep login credentials confidential
- Report suspicious activity immediately

**4.3 Breach Notification**
If a breach occurs, we will notify you within 72 hours per GDPR requirements.

## 5. DATA RETENTION

**5.1 Account Data**

**5.2 Documents**

**5.4 Marketing Data**
