## 1. SERVICES

**1.1 Subscription Services**  
LeaseAI will provide Customer with access to its lease abstraction and portfolio management software platform (the "Services") as described in the Order Form attached as **Schedule B**.

**1.2 Service Description**  
The Services include:
- AI-powered lease document extraction and abstraction
- Lease portfolio management and analytics

- CAM reconciliation tools
- Amendment tracking and resolution
- Portfolio health monitoring and alerts

**1.3 Service Availability**  
LeaseAI will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain 99.5% uptime, measured monthly. Scheduled maintenance is excluded from uptime calculations.

## 2. TERM & TERMINATION

**2.1 Initial Term**  
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term specified in the Order Form (typically 12 months).

**2.2 Renewal**  
The Agreement automatically renews for successive renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") equal in length to the Initial Term, unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current term.

**2.3 Termination for Cause**  
Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party:
- Materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure within 30 days of written notice
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

**2.4 Termination for Convenience**  
Customer may terminate for convenience upon 60 days' written notice, subject to payment of fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

**2.5 Effect of Termination**  
Upon termination:
- Customer's access to the Services will cease
- Customer may export its data within 30 days
- LeaseAI will delete Customer Data per the Data Processing Agreement (Schedule A)
- Accrued fees and obligations survive termination

## 3. FEES & PAYMENT

**3.1 Subscription Fees**  
Customer will pay LeaseAI the subscription fees specified in the Order Form.

**3.2 Payment Terms**  
- Invoices are issued at the start of each billing period (monthly or annually)
- Payment is due within 30 days of invoice date
- Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month (or maximum rate permitted by law)

**3.3 Fee Increases**  
LeaseAI may increase fees upon renewal by providing at least 60 days' written notice. If Customer does not accept the increase, Customer may terminate without penalty.

**3.4 Taxes**  
Fees exclude all taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes (excluding taxes based on LeaseAI's income).

**3.5 Suspension for Non-Payment**  
If Customer is more than 15 days late in payment, LeaseAI may suspend access to the Services upon 10 days' written notice. Access will resume upon payment.

**4.1 Customer Data Ownership**  
Customer retains all ownership rights to Customer Data. Customer grants LeaseAI a limited license to use Customer Data solely to provide the Services.

**4.2 Data Security**  
LeaseAI will implement reasonable security measures to protect Customer Data, as described in the Information Security Policy and DPA (Schedule A).

**4.3 Data Processing Agreement**  
The Data Processing Agreement (DPA) attached as **Schedule A** governs LeaseAI's processing of personal data contained in Customer Data.

**4.4 Data Backup & Recovery**  
LeaseAI maintains daily backups of Customer Data with 30-day retention. Backups are for disaster recovery purposes only and not intended for individual data recovery.

**4.5 Data Export**  
Customer may export its data at any time in CSV, Excel, or PDF format.
