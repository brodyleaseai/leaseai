## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions from GDPR**  
Terms such as "Personal Data," "Data Subject," "Processing," "Controller," and "Processor" have the meanings set forth in the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

**1.2 Additional Definitions**
- **"Services":** The lease abstraction, analysis, and portfolio management software provided by LeaseAI
- **"Customer Data":** All data uploaded, submitted, or generated by Customer through the Services
- **"Lease Data":** Commercial lease documents and extracted lease information
- **"Sub-processor":** Third-party service providers engaged by LeaseAI to assist in providing the Services

---

## 2. SCOPE & APPLICABILITY

**2.1 Relationship**  
Customer is the Data Controller and LeaseAI is the Data Processor with respect to Personal Data contained in Lease Data.

**2.2 Processing Activities**  
LeaseAI will Process Personal Data only to:
- Extract lease terms using AI/ML models
- Store and manage lease documents
- Generate reports and analytics
- Provide customer support
- Ensure system security and availability

**2.3 Types of Personal Data Processed**
- Tenant names and contact information
- Property manager names and contact information
- Signatory names (lease signers)
- Broker names and contact information

**2.4 Data Subjects**
- Commercial tenants
- Landlords and property managers
- Brokers and agents
- Authorized signatories

**2.5 Processing Location**  
Data is processed and stored in: United States (primary), with backup storage in [Backup Region]

## 3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS (AS DATA CONTROLLER)

Customer represents and warrants that:

**3.1** Customer has obtained all necessary consents and permissions from Data Subjects to provide their Personal Data to LeaseAI for the purposes described in this DPA.

**3.2** Customer has a lawful basis under applicable data protection laws for providing Personal Data to LeaseAI.

**3.3** Customer will comply with all applicable data protection laws in its use of the Services.

**3.4** Customer will not upload to the Services any Personal Data classified as "Special Category Data" (sensitive personal data) under GDPR unless explicitly agreed in writing.

---

## 4. LEASEAI OBLIGATIONS (AS DATA PROCESSOR)

**4.1 Processing Instructions**  
LeaseAI will Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, as set forth in this DPA and the Master Services Agreement.

**4.2 Confidentiality**  
LeaseAI will ensure that all personnel with access to Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

**4.3 Security Measures**  
LeaseAI implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Access controls and authentication (including MFA)
- Regular security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Audit logging of all access to Personal Data
- SOC 2 Type II certification (in progress)

**4.4 Sub-processors**  
LeaseAI may engage Sub-processors to assist in providing the Services. Current Sub-processors are listed in **Appendix A**. LeaseAI will:
- Notify Customer at least 30 days before adding or replacing a Sub-processor
- Ensure Sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA
- Remain fully liable for Sub-processor performance

**4.5 Data Subject Rights**  
LeaseAI will, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify Customer if it receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise their rights (access, rectification, erasure, etc.). Customer is responsible for responding to such requests. LeaseAI will provide reasonable assistance.

**4.6 Data Breach Notification**  
LeaseAI will notify Customer without undue delay (and in any event within 72 hours) of becoming aware of a Personal Data breach. Notification will include:
- Nature of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

**4.7 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)**  
LeaseAI will provide reasonable assistance to Customer in conducting DPIAs, if required.

**4.8 Audits & Inspections**  
Customer may, upon 30 days' written notice and no more than once per year, audit LeaseAI's compliance with this DPA. LeaseAI may fulfill this obligation by providing:
- SOC 2 Type II report
- Completed security questionnaire
- Other audit evidence

**4.9 Data Retention & Deletion**  
Upon termination of the Services or upon Customer's written request, LeaseAI will:
- Delete or return all Personal Data within 30 days
- Certify in writing that deletion is complete
- Exception: Data may be retained as required by law or for backup purposes (maximum 90 days)

## 5. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

**5.1 Transfers Outside EEA**  
If Customer is located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and Personal Data is transferred to LeaseAI in a country without an adequacy decision, the parties agree to rely on **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** as approved by the European Commission (Module Two: Controller-to-Processor).

**5.2 Additional Safeguards**  
LeaseAI implements supplementary measures including encryption, access controls, and data minimization to ensure adequate protection for international transfers.
